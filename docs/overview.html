Autonomous support and incident agent

Incident Resolver

A ticket comes in from a customer or a tester. The agent reads the logs, queries the database, searches past incidents, and reads the source code. It answers, proposes a data fix, or patches the bug. A human approves every write.

The problem

Most support tickets are not engineering problems. The few that are cost an engineer an afternoon, and what they learn is lost by the next one.

questionAlready answered somewhere

"How do I get the images back?" A help article covers it. It still waits in the same queue.

looks brokenWorking as designed

A declined card shown as "Checkout failed". The reason is in the logs; the customer and the support agent cannot see it.

real defectNeeds logs, database and code at once

A stale total, a discount taken twice. Someone opens three tools, and repeats an investigation done last month.

Incident Resolver tells the three apart, gathers evidence from every source in parallel, and comes back with a verdict and the evidence behind it. What it cannot settle alone goes to a person with the evidence attached.

Try it in five minutes

Everything below happens in the two web apps. Nothing to install.

  1. Open ShopLite and pick Ava Chen in the "Signed in as" picker at the top.

  2. Break a checkout. Add a product, go to checkout, and pay with card 4000 0000 0000 0002. The toast says only "Checkout failed" and shows a trace id.

  3. Press "Report a problem" on the toast. A ticket opens in the portal with Ava's email, the trace id, and what she was doing. Nothing to retype.

  4. Open the portal. The ticket is at the top of the queue. Click it and watch the timeline fill in live: Triage, three investigators, tool calls with their results, then the Verdict.

  5. Decide on the approval card. The customer Reply stops for you. Approve it as written, or edit the wording first.

  6. Back in ShopLite, open "My tickets". Ava reads the Reply you approved.

  7. Explore the rest. Each ticket links to its agent trace in Langfuse and its ShopLite request in Grafana. Any closed ticket has a Re-run button. An escalated one has a Resolve manually form, and what you write there becomes an Incident. Reset on the Demo panel puts everything back, keeping the knowledge base.

Filing a ticket by hand

Not every problem starts on the storefront. The portal has its own form for a tester's report, which is how the stale-total and images scenarios below are filed.

  1. Click "File a ticket" in the portal header, or open incident-portal.nirajk.dev/tickets/new.

  2. Write a summary and what went wrong in your own words. Name the customer account you were testing on, such as Ava Chen, so the Data Investigator knows whose rows to look at.

  3. Optionally add steps to reproduce and a ShopLite trace id, copied from an error toast. A trace id lets the Log Investigator jump straight to the failed request instead of searching for it.

  4. Submit. The ticket is filed as a tester ticket, the run starts at once, and the portal opens its timeline. A tester ticket has no customer Reply to approve, so it closes on its own unless a data fix or pull request stops at the gate.

Planted bugs to test it with

Five scenarios, one per category the agent has to tell apart. Four are provoked from the storefront; the last from the portal's Demo panel.

Images not loading

question

Not a bug at all, and that is the test. File a customer ticket saying the catalog pictures have been broken grey boxes since the sale started.

Where

Portal → File a ticket, or the same words from ShopLite.

Expected

answered in seconds from the clear-cache help article. Timeline shows Triage only.

Card declined, shown as "Checkout failed"

user_error

The mock gateway declines any card ending in 0002. The store hides the reason; the logs and the payments row have it.

Trigger
  1. Add anything to the cart, go to checkout.
  2. Pay with 4000 0000 0000 0002.
  3. Press "Report a problem" on the toast.
Expected

answered. The Log Investigator finds the 402 and the warn line by trace id; the Data Investigator finds the payments row, scoped to Ava. The Reply says the bank declined it and nothing was charged.

Cart total stale after removing an item

Crash on empty-cart checkout

code_bugfound by Sentinel

Checkout prices the cart before checking it is empty, and pricing throws on an empty list, so the route answers 500. Nobody has to report this one.

Trigger
  1. In the portal press Ctrl + Alt + D to open the Demo panel.
  2. Click Simulate traffic: thirty seconds of empty-cart checkouts.
  3. Watch the queue. Grafana shows the error rate climbing.
Expected

Sentinel sees the checkout route's server-error ratio pass 20% over a minute and opens the ticket itself, with the trace ids of the failures attached. A second burst opens no second ticket. The same pipeline then runs: Code RCA, a pull request at the gate, and fix_proposed on approval.
